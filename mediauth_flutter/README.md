# MediAuth AI

MediAuth AI is an intelligent assistant system that automates the health
insurance authorization and claims process for patients receiving treatment in
hospitals.

## 🧠 What the AI Does

- Takes patient medical and insurance data (uploaded via document or
  prescription)
- Analyzes diagnosis and treatment requirements
- Checks insurance policy rules
- Generates a pre-authorization request
- Submits it automatically to the insurer/TPA
- **If denied:** Understands the reason, generates a robust clinical appeal with
  proper justification, and resubmits automatically.
- Repeats until maximum valid insurance coverage is approved.

## 💡 Core Idea

This is a multi-agent AI system acting as an intelligent advocate around
pre-authorization and claims.

Its main goal is: 👉 To reduce delays, minimize claim rejections, and ensure
patients receive the full insurance coverage they are entitled to.
